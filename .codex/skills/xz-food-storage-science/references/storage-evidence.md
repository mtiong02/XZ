# Storage evidence policy

Use direct sources and record review dates in migrations or review notes.

- USDA FNS produce storage: https://www.fns.usda.gov/fs/produce-safety/storage
- USDA SNAP-Ed potatoes: https://snaped.fns.usda.gov/resources/nutrition-education-materials/seasonal-produce-guide/potatoes
- UK FSA current acrylamide guidance: https://www.food.gov.uk/print/pdf/node/281
- University of Minnesota Extension potato storage: https://extension.umn.edu/vegetables/growing-potatoes
- FDA safe food storage basics (refrigerated perishables, milk, meat and fish): https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/are-you-storing-food-safely
- FDA refrigerator/freezer storage chart (meat and seafood time/zone guidance): https://www.fda.gov/downloads/food/resourcesforyou/healtheducators/ucm109315.pdf

For potatoes, model pantry/cool-dark-dry storage as the household default. Do not claim refrigeration is universally unsafe: current official guidance differs by context, and UK FSA states domestic refrigeration does not materially increase acrylamide-forming potential. Treat ordinary refrigeration as an acceptable alternative and raw freezing as prohibited unless the product is specifically prepared for freezing.

The current global `牛奶` entry represents fresh or labelled-refrigerated milk because its shelf-life rule is seven days. Recommend refrigeration, allow freezing with a quality warning, and prohibit ordinary pantry storage. Shelf-stable unopened UHT milk must be modeled as a distinct package/state in a future condition-aware catalogue; package instructions override this default.
